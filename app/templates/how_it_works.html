THE GRIEVANCE LIFECYCLE

Every step is traceable.

Each status change is recorded with its timestamp and responsible account so progress is easy to follow.

  1. SubmittedThe citizen receives a reference number and can track the case.
  2. Under ReviewThe CMO reviews the grievance and its supporting information.
  3. In ProgressThe CMO forwards it to a department; its officer investigates and updates progress.
  4. Action report reviewedThe department submits its action-taken report for CMO review.
  5. Resolved or reopenedThe CMO accepts the response to resolve the case, or returns/reopens it for further action.

Citizens can view updates by signing in and opening their dashboard or tracking a reference number. Email and SMS notifications are not enabled.
