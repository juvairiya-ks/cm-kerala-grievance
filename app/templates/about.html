ABOUT US

A clearer way to manage public-service concerns.

This independent initiative demonstrates a transparent digital journey for submitting, reviewing, assigning, and tracking public-service grievances.

PURPOSE

Make the process easier to follow.

Provide a single place to record a concern, receive a reference number, and follow its progress through review and action.

VISION

A responsive, accountable service journey.

Demonstrate how clear ownership and visible status history can help people understand what happens after they raise a concern.

MISSION

Connect submission, review, and response.

Show a consistent workflow for citizens, CMO reviewers, and department officers, with a human decision-maker responsible for outcomes.

OBJECTIVES

Build trust through traceable steps.

  • Issue a reference number for each grievance.
  • Record status changes with timestamps and responsible users.
  • Support department assignment and action-report review.
  • Keep optional AI suggestions advisory and subject to human review.
Service informationThis demonstration is not an official Government of Kerala service. Do not enter real personal information or use it for urgent or official complaints.
