HELP & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

How do I submit a grievance?

Create a citizen account, sign in, and use Submit a grievance. A reference number is generated after submission.

How can I check progress?

Sign in with the same account and open your dashboard, or use Track a grievance with your reference number.

How do officer accounts get created?

Staff accounts are provisioned by the local project operator. Officers do not create accounts through public registration.

Does the AI assistant decide the outcome?

No. It provides advisory suggestions that need human review. External analysis is only enabled when configured and the citizen consents.

Will I receive email or SMS updates?

No. Updates are shown in the signed-in portal; email and SMS notifications are not enabled.

Is this an official government service?

No. This is an independent demonstration and is not affiliated with or operated by the Government of Kerala. Do not submit actual complaints, sensitive data, or urgent requests.
